==Galaxy Butterfly==
By Odie (sleepysloth.turtle025@silomails.com)

Some cute Galaxy Butterflies to visit! Galaxy Butterflies are pretty butterflies that live in the Galaxy Forest, among starflowers and various ambiguous creatures, large and small. Released for Ghost Jam 2024 :D
